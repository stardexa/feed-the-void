## Meet Nib

Nib lives in the **Void Nook**, runs a suspiciously efficient night shift, and eats unresolved issues for protein. Keep the void stable with a small act of care.

### How to care for the void

| You do | Nib does | You get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🍊 **Feed** | Nib eats a cosmic snack and gains Bits | A strange developer fortune |
| 🪀 **Play** | Nib leaves the desk and causes manageable chaos | Progress toward **Chaos QA** |
| 🌙 **Sleep** | Nib powers down under the terminal glow | Progress toward **DreamOps** |

Open one of the issue forms above, submit it, and the bot will reply before closing the issue. Your care counts toward the shared evolution meter.

### Why feed Nib?

Every interaction adds **Bits** to the collective hoard. At milestones, Nib evolves:

`Nib` → `Nibble` → `Byte` → `Small Cosmic Problem`

Voidkeepers earn a title from how they care: **Snack Engineer**, **Chaos QA**, **DreamOps**, or **Void Maintainer**.
